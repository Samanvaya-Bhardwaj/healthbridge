Most headaches are not serious and get better with rest, fluids and time. Tension headaches and migraines are the most common kinds.

## Often linked with
- Not drinking enough water
- Stress, tiredness or not enough sleep
- Skipping meals
- Long hours at a screen, or poor posture
- Too much tea or coffee, or cutting down suddenly
- Alcohol
- A cold, flu or sinus problems
- Eyesight problems

## What may help
- Drink plenty of water
- Rest in a quiet, dark room
- Eat regular meals
- Take breaks from screens, and stretch your neck and shoulders
- Keep regular sleep times
- Keep a headache diary to spot what brings it on
- Ask a pharmacist about a suitable painkiller; taking painkillers on many days a week can itself cause headaches

## See a doctor if
- Headaches keep coming back, or are getting more frequent or severe
- Painkillers don't help, or you need them on most days
- You have headaches with changes in your vision
- You are over 50 and the headaches are new
- Headaches wake you up, or get worse when you cough or strain

## Get urgent help if
- A headache comes on suddenly and is very severe, like a blow to the head
- You have a headache with a stiff neck, a fever, or a rash that doesn't fade when you press a glass on it
- You have a headache after a head injury
- You have weakness, numbness or drooping on one side of the face or body, slurred speech or confusion
- You have a fit (seizure), or lose your vision
