A cough is usually caused by a viral infection such as a cold or flu, and most coughs clear up within 3 weeks. A cough that lasts longer should be checked by a doctor.

## Often linked with
- Colds and flu
- Smoke, dust and air pollution
- Smoking
- Allergies, or mucus dripping down from the nose
- Acid reflux
- Asthma

## What may help
- Rest, and drink plenty of warm fluids
- Honey in warm water or tea can soothe a cough, but don't give honey to babies under 1 year
- Steam inhalation can loosen mucus
- Stay away from smoke and dust, and don't smoke
- Raise your head a little at night if the cough is worse when you lie down
- Ask a pharmacist about a suitable remedy

## See a doctor if
- It lasts more than 3 weeks
- It gets much worse, or keeps coming back
- You have a fever with the cough that doesn't settle
- You are losing weight without trying, or sweating at night
- You have a long-term condition such as asthma, diabetes or a lung disease

## Get urgent help if
- You cough up blood
- You have difficulty breathing, or are breathless at rest
- You have chest pain
- Your lips or face turn blue or grey
