Sneezing, a runny or blocked nose and itchy, watery eyes that keep coming back are often due to an allergy, for example to dust, pollen or pets.

## Often linked with
- House dust and dust mites
- Pollen, especially in some seasons
- Pet hair or dander
- Mould and damp
- Smoke, strong smells and air pollution
- Changes in the weather

## What may help
- Keep your home dust-free: damp-dust, and wash bedding in hot water every week
- Keep windows closed on days with a lot of pollen or pollution
- Wear a mask in dust or smoke
- Rinse your nose with salt water (saline)
- Stay away from smoke and smoky places
- Ask a pharmacist about a suitable allergy remedy

## See a doctor if
- Symptoms last for weeks, or affect your sleep or work
- Remedies from a pharmacy haven't helped
- You also have wheezing, a tight chest or a cough that won't go away
- You want to find out what you are allergic to

## Get urgent help if
- Your lips, face or tongue swell
- You have difficulty breathing, or wheezing that is getting worse
- You feel faint after contact with something you are allergic to
