Most stomach aches are not serious and get better within a few days. They are often due to indigestion, gas, constipation or a stomach bug. Pain that is severe, keeps getting worse or doesn't go away needs a doctor.

## Often linked with
- Indigestion, gas or acidity
- Constipation
- A stomach bug from food or water, often with loose motions or vomiting
- Eating too much, or food that doesn't suit you
- Period pain
- Stress or anxiety

## What may help
- Rest, and drink plenty of water in small sips
- Eat light, plain food, such as rice, khichdi or toast, when you feel like it
- Avoid spicy, oily and fried food, alcohol and fizzy drinks until it settles
- A hot water bag on your tummy can ease cramps
- Ask a pharmacist about a suitable remedy

## See a doctor if
- It doesn't get better within 2 to 3 days, or keeps coming back
- You also have a fever, or loose motions that don't stop
- It hurts when you pass urine
- You are losing weight without trying
- You are pregnant

## Get urgent help if
- The pain is sudden and severe, or keeps getting worse
- Pain starts around the belly button and moves to the lower right side of your tummy
- Your tummy is hard or swollen and very tender to touch
- You vomit blood, or your stools are black or bloody
- You can't keep fluids down, or you can't pass stools or wind
