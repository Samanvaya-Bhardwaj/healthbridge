Acne is common, especially in teenagers and young adults. Most mild acne gets better with a simple skincare routine, though changes take a few weeks to show.

## Often linked with
- Hormone changes, such as in puberty, around periods, or with PCOS
- Oily skin and blocked pores
- Heavy or oily cosmetics
- Squeezing or picking spots
- Stress
- Some medicines
- It can run in families

## What may help
- Wash your face twice a day with a mild cleanser and lukewarm water
- Don't squeeze or pick spots, as this can leave scars
- Use oil-free products that are labelled non-comedogenic
- Remove make-up before you sleep
- Keep your hair clean and off your face
- Be patient: changes take 6 to 8 weeks to show
- Ask a pharmacist about a suitable product for mild acne

## See a doctor if
- It doesn't get better after a few months of regular care
- You have painful lumps or cysts
- It is leaving scars or dark marks
- It is affecting your confidence or mood
- It comes with irregular periods or extra hair growth
