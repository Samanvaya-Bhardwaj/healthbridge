Back pain is very common and usually gets better within a few weeks. Staying gently active helps most people recover faster than resting in bed.

## Often linked with
- Lifting or twisting awkwardly
- Sitting for long hours, or poor posture
- Not enough exercise, or weak back and tummy muscles
- Being overweight
- Stress
- Pregnancy

## What may help
- Keep moving: walking and gentle stretches help
- Avoid long bed rest
- Use a warm compress or a hot water bag
- Take breaks from sitting, and set up your desk so the screen is at eye level
- Lift by bending your knees and keeping the load close to your body
- Sleep with a pillow between your knees if you sleep on your side
- Ask a pharmacist about suitable pain relief

## See a doctor if
- It isn't getting better after a few weeks, or keeps coming back
- The pain is stopping you doing daily activities
- The pain spreads down your leg, or you have tingling or numbness in a leg
- You have a fever, or are losing weight without trying
- You are over 50 and it is new, or you have had cancer

## Get urgent help if
- You have numbness around your bottom or genitals
- You lose control of your bladder or bowels, or can't pass urine
- You have weakness in both legs, or difficulty walking
- The pain started after a serious fall or accident
