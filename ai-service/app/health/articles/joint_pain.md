Joint pain, such as knee pain, is common and often comes from strain, overuse or wear and tear. Gentle activity and keeping to a healthy weight help many people.

## Often linked with
- Overuse, or a strain from sport or exercise
- Wear and tear of the joint (osteoarthritis), which is more common with age
- Being overweight, which adds load on the knees
- An old injury
- Inflammation of the joints (arthritis)
- Long periods without moving

## What may help
- Stay active with gentle exercise such as walking, cycling or swimming
- Strengthen the muscles around the joint with simple exercises
- After a strain, rest the joint for a day or two, then start moving it again
- Use a cold pack for a new injury, or warmth for stiffness
- Keep to a healthy weight
- Wear comfortable, supportive shoes
- Ask a pharmacist about suitable pain relief

## See a doctor if
- The pain doesn't get better after 2 weeks, or keeps coming back
- The joint is swollen, or stiff for a long time in the morning
- Several joints hurt at the same time
- You can't use the joint normally, or it gives way

## Get urgent help if
- A joint is suddenly hot, red, swollen and very painful, especially with a fever
- You can't put weight on the leg, or a joint looks out of shape after an injury
- You have severe pain after a fall or accident
