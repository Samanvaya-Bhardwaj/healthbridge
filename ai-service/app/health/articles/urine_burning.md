A burning feeling when passing urine is often caused by a urine infection (UTI), especially in women. A doctor can check your urine and advise on treatment.

## Often linked with
- An infection in the bladder
- Not drinking enough water
- Holding urine for a long time
- Sex
- Pregnancy
- Diabetes

## What may help
- Drink plenty of water through the day
- Don't hold urine: go when you need to
- Wipe from front to back after using the toilet
- Pass urine after sex
- Avoid perfumed soaps and sprays in the genital area
- Wear cotton underwear

## See a doctor if
- It doesn't start to get better within 2 days, or keeps coming back
- You are a man, you are pregnant, or it is a child
- You see blood in your urine
- You have diabetes or a weakened immune system

## Get urgent help if
- You have a high fever with shivering, or pain in your side or lower back
- You are vomiting, or feel very unwell or confused
- You can't pass urine at all
