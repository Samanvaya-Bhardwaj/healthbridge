A sore throat is usually caused by a virus, such as a cold or flu, and normally gets better within a week without antibiotics.

## Often linked with
- Colds and flu
- Smoking, or second-hand smoke
- Shouting, or using your voice a lot
- Dry air, dust or pollution
- Acid reflux
- Tonsillitis

## What may help
- Gargle with warm salt water (not for young children)
- Drink warm fluids and plenty of water
- Honey in warm water can soothe the throat, but don't give honey to babies under 1 year
- Rest your voice
- Stay away from smoke and smoky places
- Ask a pharmacist about a suitable remedy

## See a doctor if
- It doesn't get better after a week
- You get sore throats often
- You have a high fever, or white patches on your tonsils
- Your voice has been hoarse for more than 3 weeks
- You have a weakened immune system

## Get urgent help if
- You have difficulty breathing, or noisy breathing
- You can't swallow your saliva, or you are drooling
- There is severe swelling in your throat or neck
