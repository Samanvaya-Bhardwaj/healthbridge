Itching and rashes are common and often come from irritation or an allergy. Many settle within a few days once the cause is avoided.

## Often linked with
- Dry skin, especially in winter
- Soaps, detergents, perfumes or new cosmetics
- An allergy to a food, plant, metal or medicine
- Heat and sweating (prickly heat)
- Insect bites
- Fungal infections in skin folds
- Eczema

## What may help
- Use a mild, fragrance-free soap, and moisturise after bathing
- Try not to scratch, and keep your nails short
- Wear loose, cotton clothes
- Stop using any new product that may have caused it
- Keep skin folds clean and dry
- A cool compress can ease itching
- Ask a pharmacist about a suitable cream or remedy

## See a doctor if
- It lasts more than 2 weeks, or keeps coming back
- It spreads, or covers a large area
- It is painful, blistering, oozing or looks infected
- The itching is stopping you sleeping
- You have a fever with the rash

## Get urgent help if
- Your lips, face, tongue or throat swell
- You have difficulty breathing or swallowing
- A rash doesn't fade when you press a glass on it, and you have a fever or feel very unwell
- Large areas of skin blister or peel, or there are sores in the mouth or eyes
