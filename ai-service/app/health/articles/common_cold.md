A cold is a common viral infection of the nose and throat. It usually gets better by itself in about a week, though a cough can last longer. Antibiotics don't help a cold.

## Often linked with
- Viruses spread by coughs, sneezes and hands
- Close contact with someone who has a cold
- Touching your nose or eyes with unwashed hands
- Being run down, or not getting enough sleep

## What may help
- Rest and sleep
- Drink plenty of warm fluids, such as warm water, soups or herbal tea
- Steam inhalation or a warm shower can ease a blocked nose
- Gargle with warm salt water for a sore throat
- Wash your hands often, and cover coughs and sneezes
- Ask a pharmacist about a suitable remedy for the symptoms

## See a doctor if
- It doesn't start to get better after 10 days, or lasts more than 3 weeks
- It suddenly gets worse
- You have a high fever, or feel hot and shivery
- You have a long-term condition such as asthma, diabetes, or heart or lung disease
- You have pain in your face, ear or teeth that doesn't go away

## Get urgent help if
- You have difficulty breathing, or are breathless at rest
- You have chest pain
- You cough up blood
- You feel confused or very drowsy
