Period pain is common and is usually felt as cramps in the lower tummy, sometimes spreading to the back and thighs. It usually starts with the period and lasts 1 to 3 days.

## Often linked with
- The womb muscles tightening during a period, which is normal
- Sometimes conditions such as endometriosis or fibroids, especially if the pain is severe or new
- Stress
- Smoking

## What may help
- Put a hot water bag or warm compress on your tummy
- Gentle exercise, such as walking, stretching or yoga
- A warm bath
- Gentle tummy massage
- Rest, and drink enough water
- Ask a pharmacist about a suitable painkiller

## See a doctor if
- The pain is severe, or stops you doing daily activities
- The pain is new or has changed, especially if you are over 25
- Your periods are very heavy or irregular, or you bleed between periods or after sex
- You have pain during sex, or at other times of the month

## Get urgent help if
- You have sudden, severe pain in your lower tummy, especially if you might be pregnant
- You have heavy bleeding with dizziness or fainting
- You have pain with a high fever
